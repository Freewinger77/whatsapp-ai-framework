Anti-ban pipeline

Fifteen layers of protection. Every send passes through them.

The pipeline is opinionated by default and tunable per-instance. Conservative, moderate, and aggressive presets ship out of the box. Hot-reload on config change — no socket bounce.

CADENCE

Human Cadence Engine

Send timing modeled on real typing rhythm — Gaussian-jittered delays, identical-message dedupe, new-chat penalties, burst allowance. No robotic intervals.

WARMUP

Onboarding Ramp

New numbers ramp from 20 messages on day 1 to ~680 on day 7, then unrestricted. Re-warmup automatically after 72h inactivity.

RISK

Risk Telemetry

Real-time risk score 0–100 across disconnects, 401 / 403 / 463 errors, retry rate, decrypt failures. Bucketed low / medium / high / critical.

SAFETY

Circuit Breaker

Auto-pause at risk threshold. The instance pauses itself before the platform pauses it for you. Resume manually or wait for cooldown.

DETECTION

Timelock Sentinel

Detects WhatsApp's 463 reachout-timelock the moment it fires. New-contact sends quarantined; existing chats keep flowing.

QUALITY

Engagement Guard

Tracks reply ratio per recipient. Chronically unanswered contacts (<10%) blocked for 24h before they flag your number to WhatsApp.

GRAPH

Social Graph Compliance

Requires 1:1 handshake before bulk and group sends. Twelve-hour group lurk after join. Daily strangers cap. Behaves like a real network.

PRESENCE

Behavioral Choreographer

Typing presence at realistic WPM (45 ± 15), think pauses, circadian rhythm — slow at night, near-silent 02:00–06:00 local time.

RETRY

Spiral Detector

Classifies every retry by reason (no-session, bad-mac, server-429, …). Detects the moment a message enters a fail loop and stops it.

RECONNECT

Soft Re-entry

After reconnect, throughput ramps from 10% to 100% over 60 seconds. No tell-tale burst-flood that triggers post-reconnect rate limits.

CRYPTO

Identity Resolver

Auto-canonicalizes LID ↔ PN identity mappings on every inbound event. Eliminates Bad MAC and decrypt errors that would otherwise trigger retries.

CRYPTO

Crypto Health Monitor

Tracks decrypt success / failure ratio per session. More than three Bad MACs in 60 seconds flips the instance to DEGRADED and fires an alert.

CONNECT

Ghost Handshake

Random browser fingerprint at every connect, persisted per-instance for stickiness. Delayed presence broadcast — no "I just came online" tell.

RECOVERY

Smart Reconnect

Typed disconnect codes with bounded backoff. 401 fatal, 408 recoverable, 429 throttled, 503 retry — each handled correctly.

ALERTS

Real-time Alerts

Per-instance webhook plus global Telegram / Discord / HTTP alerts on any risk-state transition. Debounced upward, immediate on critical.

FAQ

The questions everyone asks.

How fast is onboarding from zero to a live number?

Country pick → instance creation is instant. The slow part is WhatsApp's own QR / pairing-code link, which is 5–30 seconds in our experience. Add the 7-day warmup before you hit full throughput, but you're sending real messages from minute one.

What's your ban rate?

Targeted at <1% across our managed fleet, and we measure this continuously. The honest answer: nobody can promise zero bans on WhatsApp because the platform changes its enforcement constantly. Viperclaw is engineered to give you the longest possible runway — behavioral protection, residential proxies, real-time risk telemetry — and to detect platform pushback before it escalates to a ban.

How much can I send per day?

Days 1–7: ramp from 20 to ~680 / day, fixed by warmup. Day 8+: depends on preset. Conservative is ~2,500 / day, moderate is ~5,000 / day, aggressive is ~8,000 / day per number. Higher than that is possible per-instance with custom tuning, but expect a higher risk score and tighter monitoring.

Do I bring my own proxies?

Default: no — we run residential proxy pools per region (5-slot pools live on UK South, UK West, Sweden, Finland today; on-demand on the rest). Each new instance auto-claims a slot. If you want your own proxies, you can override per-instance via the API and we recycle the pool slot. Live egress-IP verification proves traffic is actually going through your proxy.

Can I tune the anti-ban behavior per number?

Yes. Each instance has its own anti-ban config block — preset (conservative / moderate / aggressive) plus overrides for per-minute / per-hour / per-day caps, jitter range, circadian profile, and which modules are enabled. Hot-reload on save; no socket bounce.

Is Viperclaw multi-tenant?

The platform is built for it. Each instance is isolated end-to-end — auth folder, proxy slot, anti-ban state, webhook URL — and Battlespace can scope operators to their own subset of instances. For very large customers we can carve out a dedicated Battlespace and dedicated regional apps.

How do I get notified when something goes wrong?

Per-instance webhook for routine events (incoming messages, status updates). Global webhook + Telegram + Discord channels for high-priority events: risk transitions to high or critical, timelock detections, decrypt-degraded sessions, instance bans. Risk transitions are debounced upward (no spam) and immediate on critical.

What if a region goes down?

Regions are isolated — an outage in one doesn't propagate. New onboardings route around the affected region automatically. Existing instances on the affected region wait for it to recover (we don't relocate active sessions because that triggers WhatsApp re-pairing). Battlespace surfaces the outage with offline status dots immediately. RPO/RTO targets are part of Scale tier SLA.
